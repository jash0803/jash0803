## About Me:
AI Engineer who builds production-grade LLM systems that bridge the gap between research and real-world deployment.

At Binocs Labs, I designed advanced RAG pipelines with query transformations, LLM reranking, and agentic workflows. My obsession with observability led me to contribute to Arize Phoenix (9.5k+ stars) and OpenInference (950+ stars), shipping support for OpenAI Responses API, fixing Anthropic streaming instrumentation, and implementing end-to-end OpenTelemetry tracing. At Substance AI, I built a LangGraph multi-agent underwriting system that cut processing time from 5 days to under 5 minutes.

Beyond engineering, I co-authored a research paper under review at ACL 2026 on evaluating epistemic non-triviality in LLM-based hypothesis generation, led the AI Club at Dhirubhai Ambani University, and taught as an Undergraduate Teaching Assistant for the Machine Learning course.

I am driven by the belief that great AI systems are not just technically sound but also observable, reliable, and impactful at scale.
Open to full-time AI Engineer roles. Feel free to reach out or connect if you are working on something interesting in the LLM or agentic AI space.
